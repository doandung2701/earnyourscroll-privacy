Privacy Policy for Earn Your Scroll

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Publisher: ACS JSC · Package ID: vn.acstech.earnyourscroll

Earn Your Scroll is designed so your focus habits stay on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what data stays local, what device permissions are used, and what third-party advertising services operate in ad-supported builds.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your locked-app selections, earned minute balance, focus session timers, micro-goals, completed goal history, streaks, urge journal entries, and desk key hash are stored strictly on your device in local app preferences. Earn Your Scroll does not require an account and does not upload your app usage, focus history, or journal entries to developer servers.

2. Permissions Used (Android)

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally on your device to detect when a locked app comes to the foreground so the Earn Your Scroll shield can appear. App usage history is never uploaded or shared.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to show the shield screen and floating unlock/focus countdown pill.
  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService, optional): Used only if you enable hiding notifications during active focus sessions; intercepted notifications are dismissed locally and their contents are never read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used locally for 1-minute unlock warnings and timed re-locking.

3. Permissions Used (iOS)

  • Screen Time / Family Controls: Used on-device via Apple's frameworks to shield or hide chosen apps during focus and unlock windows.
  • Apple Health (optional): If you enable Move to scroll, only today's step count is read on-device to award scroll minutes; health data is never uploaded or used for advertising.
  • Camera (optional): Used locally only to scan a Desk Key QR code; no photos or video are stored or uploaded.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

In Google Play and ad-enabled releases, Earn Your Scroll displays optional user-initiated rewarded ads powered by Google AdMob. Requests are configured for non-personalized ads (npa=1). Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process your device's Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, app interaction events, and diagnostic/crash data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and analytics as governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Earn Your Scroll is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your focus and app-locking state resides solely on your device, clearing app data or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data. For questions about this policy, contact us at dungbuiforwork@gmail.com.
